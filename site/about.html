About Argos Monitor

Argos Monitor tracks news and crisis reporting across 87 countries, comparing domestic press, local-language diaspora outlets, and international coverage side by side. Every headline and quote links directly to the original article; nothing is paraphrased or written by an LLM.

Built by Károly Gergely in Python, SQLite, ONNX Runtime, and vanilla JS, automated on GitHub Actions and served on Cloudflare Pages. Source on GitHub (MIT).

Core principles

  1. No statement without a source: Every headline and quote links back to the stored article it came from. Nothing is summarised or invented.
  2. No LLM in the pipeline: Clustering, topic sorting, ranking, and quote extraction run on multilingual embeddings and explicit rules.
  3. Reprints don’t count twice: State wire copies and syndicated articles (≥ 0.95 similarity) are collapsed into one, so 20 identical reprints count as a single outlet.
  4. Domestic, diaspora, and international side by side: Each story shows where coverage comes from, with Official and Reported tags separating state wires from independent reporting.
  5. Elections and crises first: National elections, referendums, active conflict, and major disasters get dedicated tracking and top placement in briefs.

How the 11-stage pipeline works

A scheduled GitHub Actions job runs the full pipeline daily at 21:17 UTC and deploys static JSON bundles to Cloudflare Pages, with 4-hourly feed snapshots (01:00–17:00 UTC) so breaking articles are captured before fast RSS queues rotate. Each desk tracks 10–15 representative outlets rather than attempting exhaustive archival.

01 · IngestPolls 1,390+ RSS/Atom feeds via HTTP 304 conditional GETs and ingests verified GDELT 2.0 conflict URLs into SQLite.
02 · ExtractPulls full article text with Trafilatura where accessible.
03 · NormaliseApplies Unicode NFC normalisation (essential for Vietnamese diacritics and Khmer) and strips RSS emojis.
04 · RelevanceDrops sports/lifestyle noise and checks country mentions on global feeds.
05 · EmbedEncodes headlines and leads locally on CPU via ONNX Runtime (intfloat/multilingual-e5-small).
06 · ClusterGroups articles into stories via agglomerative cosine clustering, merges centroids, and flags wire reprints.
07 · ClassifyAssigns each story to one of 6 policy domains using URL/section rules and bilingual embedding anchors.
08 · Policy & RankScores stories by independent outlet count, scope diversity, recency, surge detection, and policy weights (themes.yaml).
09 · Excerpts & VerifyPicks 2–3 informative sentences per story and asserts each is an exact substring of the stored article text.
10 · Offline TranslationTranslates non-English headlines on CPU with MarianMT + entity glossaries, caching results in SQLite.
11 · Signals & ExportMerges GDELT, ACLED, GDACS/USGS/NASA EONET, UN/ICG/HRW/LWJ/MSF monitors, travel advisories, and social context into static bundles.

Methodology & technical details

Expand any section below for exact thresholds, source classifications, and architecture details.

Verbatim quotes & wire de-duplication
01 · Substring check Candidate pull-quotes from Trafilatura are checked with assert quote in stored_article_text. If extraction or punctuation normalisation alters a single character, the quote is dropped.
02 · Extractive summaries Story cards show the representative headline plus 2–3 verbatim excerpts from different outlets, each with publisher, timestamp, and direct source link.
03 · Wire de-duplication Lead sentences with cosine similarity ≥ 0.95 (e.g., VNA, MTI, ČTK, or Yonhap reprints) are collapsed as syndicated copies.
04 · Independent outlet counts Prominence is scored by distinct independent publishers, not gross article volume, so coordinated wire reprints do not inflate diversity.
Offline machine translation (MarianMT + glossaries)
01 · Local MarianMT models Headlines across 20+ languages are translated on CPU using Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-* models inside the runner—no paid translation APIs.
02 · Entity glossaries Post-processing glossaries fix common low-resource artifacts, such as Sino-Vietnamese names being rendered into Chinese pinyin (e.g. Tô Lâm).
03 · SQLite translation cache Translations are cached by article_id in SQLite for instant O(1) lookup on incremental runs.
04 · Original text stays primary Original headlines remain accessible via [Show original], and extracted quotes are never machine-translated server-side.
Source alignment, political leaning & factuality
01 · Alignment (ownership & control) State-controlled (statutory agencies/ministry organs), Pro-government (private media allied with the ruling coalition), Public broadcaster (chartered public-service media), Independent (free of state ownership), and Opposition (dissident, diaspora, or exile press).
02 · Editorial leaning Calibrated against Media Bias/Fact Check, AllSides, and Reuters Institute (Left to Right). In state-directed systems (e.g. Vietnam, China), outlets are tagged State-aligned.
03 · Factuality Very High (major wires with primary-source standards), High (credible mainstream reporting), Mostly Factual (factual core with partisan framing), and Mixed (subject to censorship or selective omission).
04 · Official corroboration tags Separates state messaging from independent reporting:
Official Any state-controlled outlet covers the cluster
State-Dominated Over 70% of corroborating coverage comes from state media
Crisis, conflict & security tracks
Track 1 · Live streams, field monitors & sensors Ingests GDELT 2.0 CAMEO conflict codes (18, 19, 20, 145) directly into SQLite, optional ACLED battle/fatality records, field reports from UN Peace & Security, Crisis Group, HRW, LWJ, and MSF, US State Dept / UK FCDO advisories, and GDACS / USGS M4.5+ / NASA EONET hazard feeds.
Track 2 · Kinetic conflict classification Separates active violence from routine defence diplomacy into three tracks: Armed Clash & Strike, Insurgency & Terror, and Civil Unrest & Crackdown.
Track 3 · Religious & sectarian sensitivity Tags stories only when a confessional identity or sacred site co-occurs with active violence or coercion (Judaism, Christianity, Islam, Other Faiths & Sacred Sites).

Delta analysis (“What Changed?”) & policy ranking
01 · Story velocity Compares the active window (24h or 7d) against the prior equal period:
NEWZero articles in the prior period
SURGE +ΔNGain ≥ 3 articles and velocity ≥ 1.75×
COOLINGDrop ≥ 3 articles and velocity ≤ 0.40×
02 · Scope escalation & state drift Flags domestic stories picked up abroad or dominated by state media:
INTL PICKUPPicked up by international wire services
DIASPORA PICKUPReported by diaspora or exile outlets
STATE SURGEState media share gain ≥ +30%
03 · Six policy domains & macro shifts Classifies stories into Politics & Governance, Diplomacy & Foreign Policy, Economy & Trade, Defence & Security, Society & Rights, and Environment & Energy, tracking period-over-period attention shifts (ΔS) and bilateral/multilateral cross-coverage.
04 · Ranking formula & noise suppression Score = Coverage × (0.6 + 0.5 × Policy) + 1.2 × Policy + Election_Bonus. Corroboration across 3+ and 5+ independent outlets amplifies rank; local crime, traffic, and celebrity items get low weights (0.05–0.2); elections get top weight (1.0) and ELECTION PRIORITY placement.
05 · Overview triage & desk directory The Overview page prioritizes active crisis alerts, Elections & ballot coverage, and 24-hour velocity ahead of 7-day volume. Desk lead stories and front-page picks prefer substantive policy/news categories over other items, and the monitored countries directory sorts by Attention (alerts, elections, and ↑ surging stories first) with instant Country jump and keyword search.
Auxiliary real-time signals & zero-tracking privacy
01 · Public Telegram & Bluesky feeds Polls public t.me/s/ web previews and open AT Protocol feeds (original posts from the last 7 days, replies and reposts excluded, cross-post duplicates merged) without API keys.
02 · Reddit & search surges Fetches regional subreddits in small desk batches (filtering out tourist/personal fluff with theme embeddings) and Google Trends surges (filtering out sports scores, horoscopes, and spam, linked to their news context).
03 · Unverified quarantine Auxiliary signals sit in a separate drawer marked unverified and never mix into verified story clusters or outlet counts.
04 · Static delivery & no tracking Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS on Cloudflare Pages. No cookies, accounts, or analytics; outbound links enforce rel="noopener noreferrer" and Referrer-Policy: no-referrer.
