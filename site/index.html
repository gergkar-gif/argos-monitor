How to read the stamps

Reported
One or more outlets published it. Nothing more is claimed.
Official
At least one source is a government or Party outlet. This means "officially stated", not "independently verified".
Not in domestic press
Covered by outlets abroad that publish in the country's language, or by international outlets, but by no outlet inside the country.
7 independent outlets
Outlets counted once each. Reprints of the same text by another outlet are not counted as separate sources.

Stories are grouped and sorted by topic automatically, so some will be misplaced. Quotes are copied from the original article and checked against it by the machine. To read a headline in English, use your browser's translate feature.
