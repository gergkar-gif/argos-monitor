How to read the tags

Reported
One or more outlets published it. Nothing more is claimed.
Official
At least one source is a government or ruling-party outlet. This means "officially stated", not "independently verified".
Not in domestic press
Covered by outlets abroad that publish in the country's language, or by international outlets, but by no outlet inside the country.
New Growing Quiet
From when the articles were published: all in the last 24 hours, half or more in the last 24 hours, or none in the last 24 hours.
7 independent outlets
Outlets counted once each. Reprints of the same text by another outlet are not counted as separate sources.
