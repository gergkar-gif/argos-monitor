How to read the labels

Official source
At least one source is a government or ruling-party outlet. This means “officially stated”, not “independently verified”.
Mostly state media / Multiple viewpoints
Shows whether coverage is dominated by state/pro-government media or corroborated across independent and diverse outlets.
Religious sensitivity
Conflict & security event involving sectarian tensions, attacks on places of worship, or targeted pressure on religious communities.
New today · Surging · Cooling
Story velocity compared to the prior period: new emergence, sharp increase, or declining coverage.
7 independent outlets
Outlets counted once each. Reprints of the same text by another outlet are not counted as separate sources.
